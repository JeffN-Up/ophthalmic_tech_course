# Spindel Clinical Curriculum Redesign

**Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Status:** Approved design direction; implementation pending  
**Repository:** `JeffN-Up/ophthalmic_tech_course`  
**Target:** Private Spindel Eye Associates employee onboarding course  
**Public OptiTech course:** Remains separate and is not restructured by this project

## Purpose

The current private Spindel course is dominated by administrative orientation even though the Bootcamp source library contains a complete clinical ophthalmic technician curriculum. This redesign replaces that administrative-heavy sequence with a clinical-first ten-day course.

The rebuilt course will prepare a new technician to understand ocular anatomy, perform an ordered workup under supervision, obtain reliable diagnostic data, recognize urgent findings, document accurately, and follow approved Spindel protocols. Administrative orientation is compressed into Module 1 and reinforced where clinically relevant.

## Course boundaries

The platform will retain two separate courses.

### Public OptiTech Academy

- General ophthalmic technician education
- Commercial enrollment
- General clinical content without private physician-specific workflows
- Independent curriculum, media catalog, quizzes, and progress records
- Not changed by this redesign

### Private Spindel employee course

- Clinical onboarding for Spindel Eye Associates employees
- Access through the existing staff or manager authentication path
- Approved Spindel workup protocols, procedure preparation, documentation expectations, and escalation workflows
- No scheduling, appointment-placement, double-booking, or no-show instructions
- Independent curriculum, media catalog, quizzes, and progress records

Shared topics may exist in both courses, but neither course may silently inherit curriculum or progress changes from the other.

## Module standard

Each Spindel module will include:

- A clear clinical purpose
- Three to five measurable objectives
- Learner-ready instruction with practical examples
- Essential terminology and anatomy
- One canonical video overview
- One canonical audio overview
- At least one approved visual, study guide, or quick-reference aid
- A knowledge check with answer key
- A supervised skills checklist or practical worksheet
- Safety, scope, reliability, and escalation guidance
- Explicit labels for practice- or physician-specific material

Quiz completion demonstrates knowledge only. It does not establish independent clinical competency.

## Final ten-module curriculum

### Module 1 — Spindel Orientation, Privacy, Safety & Workup Foundations

**Purpose:** Introduce the practice while establishing the clinical sequence and safety habits used throughout the course.

**Content:**

- Spindel patient-first expectations and five-office team model
- Technician scope, supervision, and escalation
- HIPAA, approved systems, workstation security, and incident reporting
- Concise Veradigm arrival acknowledgement and patient identity verification
- Infection control, room turnover, equipment readiness, and documentation integrity
- Standard workup order: history, visual acuity, lensometry/refraction, tonometry, and dilation when ordered
- Why measurements must be performed in the correct sequence

This module consolidates the former welcome, HIPAA, and patient-arrival modules.

### Module 2 — Eye Anatomy, Physiology & the Visual Pathway

**Purpose:** Give technicians a working anatomical model for every test that follows.

**Content:**

- Eyelids, lacrimal system, conjunctiva, sclera, cornea, anterior chamber, iris, pupil, lens, vitreous, retina, macula, fovea, optic disc, and optic nerve
- Anterior versus posterior segment
- Aqueous production and drainage
- Accommodation, presbyopia, cataract formation, and refractive function
- Photoreceptors and the visual pathway
- Structure-to-test mapping
- Patient-friendly anatomy explanations

**Primary sources:** `The_Biological_Camera.pdf`, `Ocular_Diagnostic_Mapping.pdf`, anatomy overview media, and clinically approved Ocular Education clips.

### Module 3 — Patient History, Visual Acuity, Pupils & Motility

**Purpose:** Build the repeatable opening sequence of a dependable workup.

**Content:**

- Chief complaint and structured HPI
- Ocular, medical, medication, social, and family history
- Pertinent positives and negatives
- Distance and near acuity; corrected and uncorrected
- Pinhole testing and low-vision notation
- Adaptations for language, literacy, cognitive, developmental, or physical barriers
- Pupils, RAPD awareness, extraocular movements, alignment, and confrontation fields
- Red flags requiring immediate escalation

**Primary sources:** Clinical Pattern Recognition & HPI Cheat Sheet, visual-acuity media, pupil/motility material, and the approved workup sequence.

### Module 4 — Lensometry, Optics & Refraction Foundations

**Purpose:** Explain how existing spectacles and refractive measurements fit into the workup.

**Content:**

- Plus, minus, sphere, cylinder, axis, prism, and add power
- Manual and automated lensometry
- Neutralizing single-vision and multifocal lenses
- Marking optical centers and documenting spectacle prescriptions
- Transposition and spherical equivalent
- Autorefraction, retinoscopy context, and subjective-refraction workflow
- Common lensometry and refraction errors
- Patient positioning, coaching, and result verification
- Refraction before contact tonometry and dilation

**Primary sources:** `Lensometry_Blueprint_(3).pdf`, `Mastering_Manual_Lensometry.pdf`, Manual Lensometry Standards, `Lensometry__A_Practical_Guide.mp4`, and refraction troubleshooting material.

### Module 5 — Tonometry, IOP & Glaucoma Fundamentals

**Purpose:** Connect reliable pressure measurement with anatomy, glaucoma care, and safety.

**Content:**

- Intraocular pressure and aqueous dynamics
- Goldmann applanation principles and mire interpretation
- Tono-Pen, rebound, and non-contact methods
- Positioning, coaching, fluorescein quantity, endpoint recognition, and documentation
- Calibration awareness and manufacturer-directed cleaning
- Thick, thin, distorted, or off-center mires
- Pachymetry context and limits of informal IOP adjustment
- Open-angle versus angle-closure awareness
- When to repeat, stop, or escalate a questionable result

**Primary sources:** `Mastering_Tonometry.mp4`, Guide to Tonometry Infographic, Goldmann visual, diagnostic guides, and the Spindel protocol manual.

### Module 6 — Slit Lamp, Anterior Segment & Common Eye Disease

**Purpose:** Build understanding of anterior-segment anatomy, common pathology, and safe examination support.

**Content:**

- Slit-lamp components, illumination, positioning, and readiness
- Systematic external and anterior-segment observation
- Cornea, conjunctiva, chamber, iris, lens, lids, and lashes
- Cataract, dry eye, blepharitis, conjunctivitis, keratitis, abrasion, and keratoconus
- Shallow-chamber and acute angle-closure warnings
- Drop safety, allergies, laterality, consent, and active orders
- Documentation without diagnosis
- Infection control and escalation

### Module 7 — Retina, OCT, Optomap & Posterior-Segment Disease

**Purpose:** Teach image acquisition and disease-oriented pattern awareness without asking technicians to diagnose.

**Content:**

- Retina, macula, fovea, optic disc, vitreous, and vasculature
- OCT purpose, scan families, segmentation, signal strength, centering, and artifacts
- Optomap and fundus-image acquisition
- Diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, retinal tear/detachment, vein occlusion, and common macular findings
- Flashes, floaters, curtain or veil, and sudden vision loss
- Image quality versus interpretation
- Accurate labeling and documentation

### Module 8 — Visual Fields, Pachymetry & Specialized Diagnostics

**Purpose:** Develop reliable setup, patient coaching, quality review, and troubleshooting.

**Content:**

- Visual-field purpose, fixation, blind-spot monitoring, false positives, false negatives, and fixation losses
- Patient instruction and when to pause or restart
- Pachymetry technique and central corneal thickness
- Introductory OCT-A, corneal OCT, Pentacam/topography, and biometry
- Test selection from current physician workup orders
- Artifact recognition and documentation
- Equipment problems and escalation
- Scope restrictions and supervised competency

### Module 9 — Spindel Workup Protocols, Procedures & Pharmacology

**Purpose:** Apply the clinical foundation to approved practice-specific workflows.

**Content:**

- Provider-specific workup and testing requirements
- Cataract postoperative workups
- Medication, allergy, laterality, consent, and chart-order verification
- Instrument recognition and minor-procedure room preparation
- Chalazion, eyelid-lesion, superficial keratectomy, punctal cautery, and ptosis setup
- Sterile-field protection
- Pathology and specimen handling
- Sharps disposal, room breakdown, and documentation
- Patient communication during clinical care
- Preparation and supervised assistance versus prohibited independent decisions

Scheduling-specific instructions are excluded.

### Module 10 — Complete Patient Workup Simulation & Final Competency

**Purpose:** Integrate the course into a safe supervised workflow.

**Content:**

- Chart preparation and patient identification
- HPI and history
- Visual acuity, pupils, motility, and preliminary testing
- Lensometry and refractive sequence
- Tonometry and ordered diagnostics
- Dilation preparation when ordered
- Documentation and clinician handoff
- Red-flag response
- Infection control and room reset
- Knowledge assessment and supervised skills validation
- Continued coaching and COA preparation

The completion certificate confirms completion of the internal knowledge course. It does not represent licensure, IJCAHPO certification, or independent clinical competency.

### Media rules

- The protected Spindel catalog contains one primary video and one primary audio overview per module: 20 canonical entries.
- Infographics, PDFs, checklists, and curated short clips are supporting resources.
- The 42 Ocular Education reels are candidates requiring accuracy, duplication, relevance, attribution, and usage-rights review before publication.
- Learner-facing titles are standardized without initially renaming or deleting Drive files.
- Drive duplicates are not deleted during the rebuild.
- Private Drive file IDs remain server-side and are delivered only to authenticated Spindel learners.

## Application changes

Implementation is limited to the private Spindel path unless a shared component needs a backwards-compatible enhancement.

Expected areas:

- `client/src/data/spindelOnboarding.ts`: replace modules, lessons, and quizzes
- `client/src/pages/CourseModule.tsx`: render supporting resources and skills checklists
- Spindel media catalog: replace administrative assignments with the approved clinical map
- Tests: update curriculum, quizzes, media routing, progress, and organization isolation
- Documentation: update the Spindel setup guide and source map

The public curriculum, public quizzes, pricing, Stripe flow, public progress, and public media catalog are outside this redesign.

## Progress migration

Existing accounts remain intact. Former administrative completions cannot silently count as completion of new clinical modules.

Migration behavior:

1. Preserve employee accounts, roles, organizations, and manager relationships.
2. Preserve old progress in a dated backup or legacy snapshot.
3. Begin the new clinical completion state at zero.
4. Show a one-time explanation that the program was upgraded while account access was retained.
5. Do not modify public learner progress.
6. Display only the current clinical curriculum on the manager dashboard after rollout.

## Clinical safeguards

- Use the physician-approved Spindel protocols supplied by Jeff.
- Teach observation, measurement, preparation, documentation, and escalation.
- Do not authorize diagnosis, prescribing, medication selection, independent treatment, injections, or unsupervised invasive procedures.
- Active orders, manufacturer instructions, written policy, and direct supervision override examples.
- Verify medication and procedure details against the active chart and physician preference.
- Exclude scheduling instructions.
- Use no PHI in lessons, quizzes, screenshots, or scenarios.

## Validation

Automated checks must confirm:

- Exactly ten unique Spindel modules numbered 1 through 10
- A lesson and quiz set for every module
- No administrative-only placeholder module remains
- Anatomy, visual acuity, lensometry, tonometry, slit lamp, retinal imaging, visual fields, Spindel protocols, and capstone are represented
- One video and one audio overview per module
- Public curriculum and public media remain unchanged
- Correct organization-based course routing
- Staff and manager authentication still work
- Legacy progress cannot complete a new module
- Type checking, unit tests, production build, and HTTP smoke tests pass

Manual acceptance testing must cover:

- New and existing staff sign-in
- Manager sign-in and dashboard
- Opening all ten modules
- Playing all ten videos and ten audio overviews
- Opening supporting resources
- Completing and saving quizzes
- Progress persistence after sign-out and sign-in
- Desktop and mobile review
- Confirmation that scheduling instructions do not appear

## Rollout and rollback

1. Build on the dedicated redesign branch.
2. Create the canonical Drive asset manifest.
3. Implement curriculum, quizzes, skills checklists, and media mapping.
4. Run automated and manual validation.
5. Review staged learner and manager experiences.
6. Merge only after curriculum and media acceptance.
7. Deploy through Render.
8. Run post-deployment authentication, media, and progress checks.
9. Retain the previous deployment and database backup.
10. Roll back to the prior deployment and progress file if a critical issue appears.

## Success criteria

The redesign is complete when:

- New technicians encounter anatomy and clinical workflow early.
- Lensometry and tonometry receive substantial dedicated instruction.
- Administrative orientation occupies one module rather than most of the course.
- Every module contains meaningful ophthalmic education.
- All ten modules have a relevant video and audio overview.
- Spindel protocols appear only in the private course.
- The complete workup sequence is reinforced throughout.
- The public course remains independently operable.
- Staff and managers can sign in and retain progress reliably.
